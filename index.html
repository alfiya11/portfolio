05 · Projects

Selected work

Maine Homeowners Insurance Territory Clustering

A team project at the NAIC exploring how Maine should be divided into territories by ZIP code for homeowners insurance premium discounts. The work uses clustering to turn location data into territory groupings that regulators can interpret and act on.

ClusteringGeospatial AnalyticsInsurance

Hybrid Quantum CNN for Remote Sensing

A hybrid quantum-classical model integrating three variational quantum circuits with a CNN to classify remote-sensing imagery, achieving higher accuracy on TIFF inputs over standard JPEG via quantum feature encoding. Published in Springer.

AgriFlow: Agentic AI for Food Security

An agentic AI system (MUIDSI Hackathon 2026 Grand Finals) that enables natural-language querying across multiple USDA databases to surface food-insecurity insights. Built with a 4-member team, integrating multi-source data with predictive modeling. 4th place.

The Geography of Grief

A data-storytelling dashboard analyzing CDC overdose mortality across 3,136 U.S. counties (2003–2021). Choropleth maps, trend analyses, and regional rankings revealed that suburban counties consistently led death rates, a counterintuitive finding for public-health policymakers.

Data VizStorytellingPublic Health

County-Level Poverty Prediction

Predicted county-level poverty using broadband coverage tiers, rural-urban classification, and demographic indicators. Achieved 80% explained variance (R²) via Random Forest, identifying income, education, and broadband access as dominant predictors.
